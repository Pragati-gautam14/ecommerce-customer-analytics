Key insights

  • Customer concentration: Champions are 22.9% of customers but drive 68.1% of revenue (avg spend £5,675) → protect with a loyalty / early-access programme.
  • Win-back opportunity: 206 high-value customers are 'At Risk' (avg 131 days since last order) holding £372K of historic revenue → priority list for a reactivation campaign.
  • Retention is the main lever: only 20% of new customers buy again in month 1, yet repeat buyers (65.2% of customers) generate 93.4% of revenue.
  • Seasonality: Sep–Nov 2011 delivered 37.9% of the year's revenue → stock and staff up for Q4 from August.
  • Long tail: 22.1% of products (806 SKUs) generate 80% of revenue; cancellations cost 5.41% of gross sales (£474K).
  • Cross-sell: product pairs like teacup sets and lunch bags are bought together 7–18× more than chance (lift) → bundle and 'frequently bought together' recommendations.
